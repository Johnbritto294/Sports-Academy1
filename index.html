Sports science

The numbers behind every improvement

GPS vests, force plates and video gait analysis turn every session into feedback. Athletes see exactly what changed and why — and so do parents.

  • Live GPS load and sprint-distance tracking
  • Force-plate jump and asymmetry screening
  • Frame-by-frame technique review
  • Individual recovery and sleep guidance

Nutrition & recovery

Training is only half the job

What athletes eat and how they rest decides how quickly they improve. Every programme builds recovery into the week, not on top of it.

  • Match-day fuelling plans for every age group
  • Guided cooldown and mobility routines
  • Sleep and wellbeing check-ins
  • On-site physio and injury screening
